# Void Powers

A Forge 1.20.1 Minecraft mod that lets you play as characters from animated series: Herobrine, Entity 303, The Chosen One, The Dark Lord, Nightmare, H4CK3R and the Eternal Conflict warriors. Each has their own powers, weapons, animations and transformation.

It's on CurseForge as **Animated Universe**: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/animated-universe

Its Epic Fight add-on, **Void Powers: Epic Fight**, gives every character's weapon its own Epic Fight moveset: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/void-powers-epic-fight

## Downloads

Every version of the mod and of the Epic Fight add-on is on the [Releases](../../releases) page.

- `VoidPowers-<version>.jar`: the mod (Forge 1.20.1).
- `VoidPowersEpicFight-<version>.jar`: the Epic Fight add-on. It needs Epic Fight, Weapons of Miracles, Epic Fight Extra, Epic Fight: Dawn Day and (from 4.3.0) EpicFight-Awaken.

What changed in each version: [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) and [CHANGELOG-EpicFight-addon.md](CHANGELOG-EpicFight-addon.md).
